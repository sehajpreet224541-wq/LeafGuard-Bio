Know Your Tomato Diseases

Early Blight

A fungal disease associated with dark leaf spots and concentric ring patterns.

Late Blight

A serious disease that can cause irregular dark lesions and rapid plant damage.

Healthy Tomato

The healthy category represents leaves without the disease patterns learned by our model.

About LeafGuard Bio

LeafGuard Bio is an educational AI research project designed to explore plant disease classification using machine learning.

Our initial prototype focuses on three tomato leaf categories: Early Blight, Late Blight and Healthy.
